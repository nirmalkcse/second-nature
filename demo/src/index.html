Physics

Throw it.

While you hold it, it is your finger. When you let go, a spring takes over at exactly the speed you were moving and lands where the throw was heading. Catch it mid-flight: nothing waits for an animation to finish.

Edges

Pull past the end.

Nothing in the world stops dead. Past its limit the sheet keeps following, less and less, and never more than its own height. Flick it and it settles on the stop nearest to where momentum would have carried it.

Geometry

Corners that flow.

A standard rounded corner is a straight line that abruptly becomes a circle. A continuous corner eases into the curve, the way a machined edge does. You notice the first one. You never notice the second.

Geometry

Inner = outer − gap.

Give a child the same radius as its parent and the gap between them pinches at every corner. Subtract the padding and the two curves share a centre.

Feedback

Controls that answer.

Every press is acknowledged on the way down, before you lift. Selections are objects that slide, not highlights that teleport.

Details

Alive, not looping.

One animation on every tile moves like a machine. Three slightly different clocks per tile, each started at a random moment, take about twenty minutes to repeat. Bigger tiles tilt less, so their edges travel the same distance.

Trust

Calm numbers.

Measurements jitter. A readout that jitters with them looks broken. Show a filtered value that moves in small, orderly steps and never backtracks.
